Ruixue Milk Cream

The perfect ratio of milk foam to tea soup is
like the romance and gentleness of fresh snow falling
on the earth, quietly stealing your heart.

French Specialty

Combining Taiwanese tea with French desserts:
Inspired by French crème brûlée and lemon tart, the
brand creates signature drinks with innovative flavors
that leave a lasting impression..

Carefully Baked

We carefully select spherical tea leaves grown
at Laopi Farm in Pingtung. Through repeated roasting
and temperature control, we transform the tea into a
unique roasted aroma, resulting in a mellow br flavor and a sweet aftertaste.

Taiwanese local tea
Taiwan Selected Tea

We carefully select local Taiwanese teas, offering four varieties:
Honey Black Tea, Four Seasons Spring Tea, Milky Jin Xuan Tea, and
Roasted Tieguanyin Tea, allowing consumers with different flavor
preferences to choose their favorite taste.

French Beverage Making
French Cream Technique

Inspired by French desserts such as lemon tart, crème brûlée
and premium cocoa, four innovative mixed drinks were created
through a year of repeated testing. The unique ratio of the creamy
French milk foam ensures that even after being steeped in tea for a
longer time, it remains a rich and full-bodied milk tea, perfectly
blending Taiwanese tea drinks with French pastry techniques.

Carefully selected local materials
Exquisite Local Ingredients

Using local Taiwanese fruits, it perfectly embodies
the country's reputation as a tropical fruit kingdom.
It features plump, juicy fresh fruit pulp, Grade A perilla,
aloe vera pieces, and aiyu jelly, creating a multi-layered
taste and flavor. It contains no added spices, flavorings,
or colorings, showcasing the pure freshness of the fruit and
satisfying a brand-new feast for both the eyes and the taste buds.
